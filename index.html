Privacy Policy – FiscalLeb

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Developer: Mohammad Tarhini · Contact: mhdtar@gmail.com info@fiscalleb.com.c

Data we collect

FiscalLeb does not collect personal information such as name, email, phone number or location, and does not require an account.

To deliver push notifications, the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service), which processes a device identifier (Firebase Installation ID / push token) and basic technical device information.

The app connects to fiscalleb.com to load circulars and downloadable forms. The payroll/CNSS calculator runs on your device and its inputs are not sent to us or stored.

How we use it

Only to send you notifications related to the app.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The identifiers above are processed by Google as our service provider. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

You can stop notifications at any time from your device settings or by uninstalling the app. To request deletion, contact mhdtar@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed to children.

Disclaimer

FiscalLeb is not affiliated with any government body. Information and calculations in the app are for guidance only and do not replace a licensed accountant.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
